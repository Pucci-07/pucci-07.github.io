Two years ago, I was placed on a JPOPE team almost by chance — not something I'd actively chosen. I gave it everything I had, and we finished Top 10 out of 50 selected projects. That first taste of the event stuck with me.

This year was different. My team and I went in wanting to win — not just take part — with an AI-powered honeypot project we'd built together. The event, held at Hôtel 2 Février in Lomé on August 29, 2026, wasn't an easy room: a demanding jury, a skeptical audience to convince, and every other team pushing just as hard. Pushing through my own shyness to pitch with full confidence was its own challenge.

We took 1st place in the Cybersecurity track — and the 1,000,000 FCFA prize was a nice bonus, but the real reward was proving to ourselves we could go from "arbitrary Top 10" to "we came here to win."
